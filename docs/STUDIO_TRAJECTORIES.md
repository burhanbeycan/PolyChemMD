# MXene studio trajectory observables

A completed MD job supplies atom coordinates, stable atom identifiers and a selection manifest. The Studio UI and queue run the same analysis. Select a completed MD job and enter the neighbour cutoff and requested profile resolution, both in ångströms. In Python, `polychemmd.studio.trajectory.analyze_trajectory` also accepts a local LAMMPS trajectory and explicit selections from an independent calculation. All coordinate and cutoff lengths must use Å.

**Input geometry.** Surface planes are parallel to the first two lattice vectors. For a restricted-triclinic LAMMPS cell, the vectors form the rows of
`[[lx,0,0],[xy,ly,0],[xz,yz,lz]]`. Bounding-box extents are converted to cell extents using the [LAMMPS convention](https://docs.lammps.org/Howto_triclinic.html). The perpendicular projection is the third fractional coordinate multiplied by `lz`. Orthorhombic cells, orthorhombic/restricted-triclinic dumps with scaled or Cartesian coordinates, and gzip input are supported. The surface normal and phase assignment must be specified consistently by the geometry producer.

**Number density.** Water oxygen atoms count water molecules once. In each frame the count in each bin is divided by the instantaneous volume divided by the number of bins. `density.csv` reports the mean number density in Å⁻³ and the frame-to-frame population SD. The latter describes fluctuations. Equal-weight averaging assumes the deposited frames represent equally weighted samples in time; for irregularly spaced frames these are snapshot statistics. Bin coordinates are reduced positions with instantaneous normal distances averaged over the frames. A periodic z axis uses the reference plane as the origin and spans one full repeat of the simulation cell from −0.5 to +0.5 in reduced coordinates. A nonperiodic z axis uses the lower box edge. Fixed grid edges in reduced coordinates allow a changing cell height. For a changing box, the product of the mean density and mean bin volume generally differs from the mean count.

**Contact fraction.** A water oxygen or polymer heavy atom counts once per frame if any selected surface site lies within the requested Euclidean cutoff (default 3.5 Å). Neighbours across every periodic face are included using Cartesian lattice translations, including in tilted cells. The result divides the total counted probe-frame contacts by the total number of probe-frame observations. A missing polymer selection gives an empty polymer fraction. This geometrical criterion does not include an angular hydrogen-bond criterion. An analytic regression test compares the periodic selector against [MDAnalysis distance_array](https://docs.mdanalysis.org/stable/documentation_pages/lib/distances.html).

**Layer separation.** Two disjoint groups of reference plane atoms give a center-to-center distance along the layer normal. With a periodic normal, `contacts.csv` records both complementary paths between two planes. The reported summary uses the shorter path; asymmetric cells can contain a distinct external solvent gap. A fixed substrate retains the imposed spacing. XRD comparison requires a lamellar specimen, consistent phase and hydration, appropriate mobile-layer sampling, and independent uncertainty assessment.

**Reproducibility.** Each report contains a trajectory SHA-256, a selection SHA-256 and a source-job identifier. The source manifest identifies the plane atoms, terminating atoms, polymer heavy atoms and water oxygens by stable integer IDs. The algorithm rejects missing/duplicated IDs, inconsistent atom populations, nonfinite values and truncated frames.
